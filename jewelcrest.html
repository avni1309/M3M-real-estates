Project Overview

Where Commerce Meets Experience

M3M Jewelcrest Avenue is a premium commercial development in Sector 97, Noida, along the Noida Expressway. Conceived as a destination rather than a standalone retail block, it brings together high-street retail, F&B and a family entertainment zone with floor-wise brand zoning for maximum footfall.

Ground & Upper-Ground floors are planned for international and premium brands, the Second Floor for Indian brands, and the Third Floor as an experiential family-entertainment hub with restaurants and a food court — a mix built for sustained footfall and long-term rental value.

  • International & premium brand zone (GF & UGF)
  • Indian-brand retail floor with strong footfall
  • Family entertainment zone, restaurants & food court
  • RERA registered • assured-rental investment plans
