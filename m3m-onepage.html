Our Story

Where It All Began

M3M India was established in 2010, with an ambition to elevate urban living in the National Capital Region. The name stands for "Magnificence in the Trinity of Men, Materials & Money".

In a short span, M3M grew into a leading name in luxury real estate across Gurugram, Noida and the wider Delhi NCR — spanning ultra-luxury residences, retail high-streets, commercial space and branded developments.
