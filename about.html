Our Story

Where It All Began

M3M India was established in 2010, entering the market with an ambition to elevate the standard of urban living in the National Capital Region. The name stands for "Magnificence in the Trinity of Men, Materials & Money" — a philosophy that people, quality and capital, brought together with discipline, create landmarks that last.

In a short span, M3M grew from an ambitious newcomer to one of the leading names in luxury real estate in Gurugram and the wider Delhi NCR. The group's portfolio spans ultra-luxury residences, integrated townships, retail high-streets, commercial office space and branded developments — anchored on the premium Golf Course and Golf Course Extension corridors of Gurugram.
